Frequently asked questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a STEM lab?

A dedicated, hands-on learning environment equipped with robotics kits, electronic tools, 3D prototyping gear, and structured curricula where students explore science, technology, engineering, and mathematics through experiential projects.

Which grades can use the STEM lab?

Our STEMbot programs are designed for learners across Grades 1 through 12, featuring age-appropriate kits, progressive projects, and curriculum modules tailored for primary, middle, and senior school students.

Do you provide teacher training and lesson plans?

Yes! Implementation includes comprehensive teacher onboarding workshops, certified training modules, step-by-step teacher manuals, slide decks, and ongoing pedagogical support throughout the academic year.

How long does implementation take?

A standard lab implementation typically takes 2 to 4 weeks from space assessment to complete hardware installation, calibration, and initial teacher training.

Can the curriculum be customized to our school board?

Absolutely. Our curriculum modules are flexible and mapped to CBSE, ICSE, Cambridge, IB, and State Board academic standards and NEP 2020 experiential learning guidelines.
