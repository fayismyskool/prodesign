A global movement

Building skills. Creating opportunity.

The Global Skills Academy (GSA) is an initiative dedicated to addressing the pressing labour skills gaps and empowering individuals for a future-ready workforce. Under the umbrella of the UNESCO Global Education Coalition and in line with UNESCO's Strategy for Technical and Vocational Education and Training, the GSA is committed to supporting ten million youth and adults globally in building essential skills for improved employability by 2029.

The GSA focuses on empowering learners with key skills, including digital literacy, entrepreneurial skills and green technologies. To achieve this goal, the GSA leverages strategic partnerships and mobilizes 230 TVET institutions across 150 countries through the UNESCO and UNEVOC networks.

New programme

AI EmpowerED

Through UNESCO's Global Skills Academy, in partnership with Microsoft Elevate, KPMG International and Tablet Academy, AI EmpowerED supports TVET systems to equip educators and learners with practical and responsible AI skills for the future of work. By combining global partnerships, national training networks and certification pathways, the programme expands access to AI learning at scale, empowering teachers to drive change in the classroom and enabling learners to develop the digital competencies needed to succeed in tomorrow's economies.

Discover how AI-empowered skills are creating new pathways to inclusion, innovation and employability worldwide.
